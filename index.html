Life OS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Life OS is a private dashboard used by its owner to follow their own Instagram account, @crownclaw.lol. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. This policy explains what the app does with Instagram data.

What the app accesses

  • Basic information about the connected account (its username and follower count).
  • The account's own posts and reels, and their public numbers: views, likes, comments, shares and saves.
  • Comments that other people leave on the account's own posts, including the commenter's username, the comment text and when it was written.

How it is used

  • To show the account's performance on the owner's dashboard.
  • To list comments on the account's posts and to let the owner reply to them as the account.
  • To send the owner private notifications about new comments and milestones.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.
  • It does not access any account other than the one connected by its owner.
  • It does not post anything except replies the owner writes and sends.

Storage and retention

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer, which runs the dashboard. Account numbers are kept to show history over time. Comments are read from Instagram when needed and are not kept beyond the running dashboard's memory and the owner's backups.

Deleting data

To have any data about you removed, or to ask a question about this policy, send a direct message to @crownclaw.lol on Instagram. The data will be deleted within 30 days. Removing the app's access in Instagram (Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites) stops all further access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
